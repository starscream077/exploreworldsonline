In the age of ancient Bharat, Prince Aryavarman was the pride of Vijayapura, until a web of lies woven by jealous ministers cast him out. Stripped of his name and his throne, he vanished into the endless forest.
Years have passed. Now dark news reaches his hut: enemies close in on the kingdom, and its people cry out for their prince. But every path home is guarded by beasts, hidden pits, raging rivers and hired blades.
